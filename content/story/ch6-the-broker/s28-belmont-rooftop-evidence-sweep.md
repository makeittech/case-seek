## Investigation purpose
The old conservation studio under the rooftop glass — her studio. The original *Cartographer's Daughter* lies unrolled in lamplight; the stolen copy stands crated by the wall; Ottilie waits, hands folded, storm overhead. She has laid the whole case out on her worktable — every artifact, hers and ours — because a conservator ends a job with a full inventory. **The sweep is the summation:** each found object voices one line of the accusation (WHO → MOTIVE → METHOD → TIMELINE → KEY CONTRADICTION → CRITICAL EVIDENCE, per STORY_BIBLE §15). The player doesn't watch the case close. The player *closes it, object by object.*

## Entry beat
